# 打开 HumanIK 角色设置工具

[“骨架”(Skeleton)选项卡](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2022/CHS/Maya-CharacterAnimation/files/GUID-3250FD5A-9A95-4A1C-8017-812149FC74F7.htm)、[“定义”(Definition)选项卡](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2022/CHS/Maya-CharacterAnimation/files/GUID-630B5349-213C-414D-B6C9-D283CE319472.htm)和[“控制”(Controls)选项卡](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2022/CHS/Maya-CharacterAnimation/files/GUID-5522E13D-C958-41E7-BD84-FC160B25DE22.htm)以及[“自定义装备”(Custom Rig)选项卡](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2022/CHS/Maya-CharacterAnimation/files/GUID-5522E13D-C958-41E7-BD84-FC160B25DE22.htm)包含的 HumanIK 工具可让您设定角色以设定关键帧和重定目标。这些选项卡位于“HumanIK”窗口中。

打开 [HumanIK 窗口](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2022/CHS/Maya-CharacterAnimation/files/GUID-9921EDE1-05E2-4062-9F0D-4BADECA29226.htm)

1. 请执行下列任一操作：

  - 选择“骨架 > HumanIK”(Skeleton > HumanIK)

  - 选择“窗口 > 动画编辑器 > HumanIK”(Window > Animation Editors > HumanIK)
